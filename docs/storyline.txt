PROLOGUE:  THE DIVIDED LANDS

It was the time of early 1800s (B.S.). 
The mighty Himalayas stood silent witness to a land torn apart. Nepal did not exist. Instead, countless small kingdoms — Gorkha, Kantipur, Laltipur, Bhaktapur, Patan, and many others — each ruled by its own king, each unaware that history is about to change them forever. 
All because one ruler saw what others couldnot. 

KING PRITHIVI NARAYAN SHAH of Gorkha dreamed of a united kingdom of Nepal.

Driven by courage, ambition, and a fierce desire to preserve the culture and identity of his people, the king embarked upon a historic campaign of unification. 

Despite his determination, he was defeated not once, but twice. Each loss tested his resolve and threatened to bring his grand vision to an end. Many sacrifices were made. Countless obstacles stood in his path. Yet none proved more challenging than the fortified city of KIRTIPUR. 

The king refused to surrender.

This story begins in the aftermath of his second defeat.
