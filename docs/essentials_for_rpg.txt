Core of an RPG

  play as a protagonist
  
  guide their actions through a narrative in a fictional world

Essential elements
1. character progression and stats   
  -  levels and Exps: get stronger by completing quests defeating enemies, - level up
  -  stats and attributes: character's power, weakness and success rate in different situations

2. A Player-Driven Narrative
  -  Choices and Consequences: story that responds to decision, for example. killing or sparing a character,  which impacts the outcome of the game. type shi
